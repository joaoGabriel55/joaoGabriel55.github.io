# Surface brief: Home, Blog index, Blog post

## Scope and mode
- `/` Home: Persuade. Recruiters decide in seconds and act (email, try a project, follow).
- `/blog` and `/blog/:slug`: Read. The world owns the frame (masthead, list rhythm, title block, code setting); the reading column stays calm.

## Audience and job
Hiring managers skim for who, what was built, and how to reach him. Community readers arrive on deep links to posts. Proof comes from the talk, gem, demos, 28 merged PRs, posts, and two real LinkedIn recommendations (2021, classmates; one translated from Portuguese and labelled).

## Constraints
Reference (Webflow portfolio template) supplies only the pacing: proof strip under the hero, featured work, testimonials, writing, closing contact. Do not copy it. Football means association football. Minimal. Motion is wanted. Both themes stay.
